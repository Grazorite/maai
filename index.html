Core philosophy

MAAI

間合い

Space, timing and connection.

How we move, when we pause, and how we respond to each other. There’s room for each of us to find our own way of dancing.

In Japanese martial arts, maai (間合い) is the distance between you and the person facing you. That space changes as you respond to each other.

I’m borrowing that idea for how we dance together: noticing our space, choosing when to move or pause, and responding to the music and each other.

That’s what space, timing and connection mean for MAAI. I want us to build the skills to make our own choices, while learning and growing together.

How we’ll develop

Foundation

Foundation: strength and conditioning. Core skills: body awareness, rhythm and coordination, and footwork. Application: pickup and retention, and creative exploration. Use up and down between layers, and left and right within each layer.

Strength & conditioning

Build the strength, stability and stamina that support all of this. We’ll focus on glutes, core and upper body.

Strength and conditioning will support the three core skills: body awareness, rhythm and coordination, and footwork.

We’ll use these to pick up choreo more quickly, understand the details and remember it without following me. We’ll also explore your own style and how you make choreography.

We can adjust the physical work to suit you. Is there anything you’d like me to know?
