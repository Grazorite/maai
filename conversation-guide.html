Before we begin

First, I’d like
to hear about you.

01

What keeps you dancing?

  • What have you enjoyed most about dancing recently?
  • What kind of dancing feels most like you?
02

Where would you like to go?

  • What would you like to feel more confident doing?
  • What feels difficult right now?
03

How do you like to learn?

  • What kind of practice or feedback helps you most?
  • What would you like to learn from or share with the group?
04

What would help you take part?

  • Would a 3-hour session every two weeks work for you?
  • Could you make time for 15 minutes of practice twice a week?
  • What would help you keep making time for the group?
